# FASPEP Product Roadmap

## Purpose

FASPEP is being developed by FASRED Technologies Ltd to make digital examinations more familiar to candidates while supporting the wider assessment lifecycle.

This document describes the product's intended direction. It is not a guarantee that every capability is currently available or scheduled for a specific release.

## Product Priorities

### 1. Familiar Digital Examination Experience

- Make computer-based examinations feel closer to familiar paper-based examinations.
- Reduce unnecessary interaction barriers for candidates.
- Explore suitable ways to interact with questions, including those requiring calculations or other structured responses.

### 2. Examination Preparation and Administration

- Support examination planning and organisation.
- Improve the preparation, organisation, review, and approval of assessment materials.
- Support candidate registration and examination scheduling workflows.

### 3. Assessment and Results

- Support the collection and assessment of candidate responses.
- Develop workflows for marking, moderation, result approval, and publication.
- Support appropriate examination records and result-management processes.

### 4. Institutional Support

- Address the needs of different educational levels and assessment environments.
- Support institution-specific requirements and appropriate access controls.
- Consider academic records, progression, and related administrative workflows.

## Development Approach

FASPEP's development prioritises usability, dependable workflows, assessment integrity, privacy, and verification through testing.

The order and availability of individual capabilities may change as development, testing, and feedback progress.
