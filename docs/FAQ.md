# FASPEP — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is FASPEP?

FASPEP is a digital examinations and assessment platform being developed by FASRED Technologies Ltd. It aims to make digital examinations feel more familiar to candidates accustomed to paper-based examinations.

## What problem is FASPEP designed to address?

Moving examinations from paper to computers can introduce challenges for candidates and institutions. FASPEP is being developed to make the digital examination experience more familiar while supporting the wider examination process.

## Who is FASPEP intended for?

Its intended users include private schools, school groups, educational institutions, examination organisations, professional training providers, and employers conducting assessments.

## Which education levels does FASPEP target?

The intended scope covers early childhood education through primary and secondary education, as well as tertiary education and other assessment environments. The availability of specific features depends on implementation and release.

## Does FASPEP support the complete examination lifecycle?

FASPEP is being developed with the broader examination lifecycle in mind, including planning, question preparation, candidate registration, examination delivery, marking, moderation, results, and academic records. Not every capability should be assumed to be available in the current release.

## Is FASPEP available now?

FASPEP is under development. Contact FASRED Technologies Ltd for the latest information about availability, demonstrations, and opportunities to discuss your institution's requirements.

## Can a school or organisation request a demonstration?

Yes. Organisations can submit a product enquiry or demo request through the FASRED website.

[Request a demonstration or make an enquiry](https://fasredtech.com/contact?inquiry=sales&intent=demo)

## Can FASPEP be tailored to an institution's requirements?

Requirements vary between institutions and assessment environments. Contact FASRED to discuss your needs and determine whether they align with the product's current or planned capabilities.

## Does FASPEP represent or replace official examination bodies?

FASPEP is a product of FASRED Technologies Ltd. Its inclusion of examination-related workflows does not, by itself, imply endorsement, accreditation, partnership, or official integration with any examination body.
