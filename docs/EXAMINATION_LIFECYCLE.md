# FASPEP Examination Lifecycle

## Overview

FASPEP is a digital examinations and assessment platform being developed by FASRED Technologies Ltd. Its product direction is to support examination activities across the examination lifecycle while making the digital experience more familiar to candidates accustomed to paper-based examinations.

The lifecycle described here represents the intended product scope. Availability of individual capabilities depends on implementation, testing, and release.

## 1. Examination Planning

Prepare for an examination by defining its structure, subject or assessment areas, schedule, and applicable requirements.

## 2. Question Preparation

Organise examination questions and assessment materials. Support the preparation and review of questions before they are approved for use.

## 3. Quality Review and Approval

Provide a structured approach to reviewing examination materials, checking their suitability, and authorising them for use.

## 4. Candidate Registration

Manage candidate information and examination participation, including the relevant examination arrangements and schedules.

## 5. Examination Delivery

Provide a digital examination experience designed around familiarity and usability, with the goal of helping candidates concentrate on answering questions rather than struggling with the interface.

## 6. Submission and Assessment

Support the collection of candidate responses and the assessment of submitted work through applicable scoring, marking, and review processes.

## 7. Moderation and Results

Support the review of assessment outcomes, result validation, and the applicable approval process before results are released.

## 8. Results and Feedback

Support the communication of examination outcomes and relevant feedback in accordance with the institution's policies and authorised access.

## 9. Academic Records

The intended broader scope includes maintaining assessment history and supporting applicable academic progression and records-management workflows.

## 10. Corrections, Appeals, and Retention

The intended lifecycle also considers result corrections, appeals, auditability, and the appropriate retention of examination records.

## Designed for Different Assessment Environments

FASPEP is being developed with a broad range of potential users in mind, including:

- Private schools and school groups
- Early childhood, primary, and secondary education
- Tertiary institutions
- Examination and assessment organisations
- Professional training and certification providers
- Employers conducting recruitment or competency assessments

Specific requirements vary by organisation and assessment type. The availability of relevant workflows will depend on the capabilities implemented and released.

## Our Principle

**Technology should make examinations more accessible and familiar without compromising the requirements of the assessment.**

FASPEP is being developed toward this goal by FASRED Technologies Ltd.
